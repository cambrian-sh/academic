## Verification protocol

Nothing below is a guess. Every identifier was fetched and title-matched:

- `arXiv-API` — `export.arxiv.org/api/query?id_list=…`, title string compared
  against the returned `<title>`. 33 identifiers.
- `Crossref` — `api.crossref.org/works/{DOI}`, title compared. 7 DOIs.
- `OpenAlex` — `api.openalex.org/works?filter=…`, title compared. Used only to
  *discover* candidates; every result was then re-confirmed on arXiv or
  Crossref.
- `S2+bib` — identifier **and** title confirmed twice: once from the
  Semantic Scholar references record for the seed, and once from the seed's
  own rendered bibliography (arXiv LaTeXML or Crossref unstructured string).
  This is a weaker but genuine double-source; used for well-known IDs.
- `URL-200` — fetched, HTTP 200, no identifier to misreport.

`UNVERIFIED` appears where a claim could not be confirmed. No arXiv ID or DOI
is written anywhere in this file that was not returned by one of the above.
